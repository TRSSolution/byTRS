Understanding the Challenge

The maritime industry operates on unpredictable schedules where seafarers' availability fluctuates constantly. This creates significant inefficiencies for both seafarers trying to plan their professional development and maritime institutes attempting to schedule courses effectively.

"Traditional scheduling methods simply can't keep up with the dynamic nature of seafaring careers, creating challenges for both professionals and educators."
